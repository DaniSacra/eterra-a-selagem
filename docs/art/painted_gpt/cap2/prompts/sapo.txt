THE CREATURE: Sapo do Brejo, a grumpy round swamp toad (medium enemy that jumps and makes a wave when it lands). Big round belly, very squat wide silhouette, strong folded hind legs, short front arms, wide mouth with a grumpy expression, heavy-lidded amber-orange eyes. Mossy green back with olive and ochre warts and a few moss tufts, pale ochre-cream belly. Sitting upright on its haunches in a calm neutral pose, front arms slightly away from the belly.

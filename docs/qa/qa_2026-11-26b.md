# QA 2026-11-26b (bugs, errors, perf) — HEAD a3c1eedd

## Found / fixed
- **Perf, fixed (contained):** `--stress=300` peaked at 50 to 87 ms in `player` (`runner.step` -> `Combat.strike`) whenever a
  blow struck 100+ enemies. Cause: `BoonState.stat(name, tags)` with tags was never cached and walked the whole Blessing
  catalog (plus `level()` lookups) about 15 times per enemy struck (`Combat.hit_enemy`). Fix: a second cache keyed by
  `[stat, tags]` (`_tag_cache`), cleared together with `_cache` at every site that changes levels, Gifts, Forja, Encantos
  or granted mods. Result: `step_ms_peak` 87.4 -> 26.3 and 31.2 (two runs), `step_ms_avg` 8.13 -> 7.6.
  No gameplay change: `--smoke=30 --seed=5` and `--smoke=300 --seed=5` RESULT are byte-identical before/after (fps and
  step_ms fields stripped, md5 equal). Tests run: `test_boons` 27/27, `test_psalms` 20/20, `test_e4_ui` 16/16.
- **Test added:** `tests/test_hub_walk.gd` walks the hero (Arena.resolve, as Player does) in straight lines between every
  pair of the 7 stations through the new stairs: never outside the floor, never inside a building footprint, height
  steps never taller than a tread (0.2 m). Passes.
